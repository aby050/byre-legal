Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 September 2026

Byre is a screen-time and focus app. It is built to work without collecting your data, and this policy describes exactly what that means in practice.

The short version: Byre has no servers of its own. What you do in the app stays on your device. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information, and there is no advertising or tracking in the app.

Information stored on your device

The following is written to your device's local storage and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your onboarding answers, goal, chosen theme and unlock difficulty
  • Which apps and categories you have chosen to block
  • Your focus sessions, streaks, reflections and progress history
  • Your Bailout balance
  • Any progress photo or message to your future self you choose to record
  • Optional sign-in details, if you sign in — these are stored locally; there is no account server

Deleting the app, or using Delete Account inside it, removes all of this permanently. We cannot recover it, because we never had it.

Screen Time and blocked apps

On iOS, Byre uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework to block apps. When you choose apps to block, Apple returns an opaque token rather than the names of the apps. Byre is never told which apps you selected — only that something is selected and should be shielded. Your usage data stays on the device and is processed by Apple's frameworks, not by us.

Camera

During a physical unlock challenge, the camera is used to count repetitions using a pose model that runs entirely on your device. No video or image from the challenge is recorded, saved, or transmitted anywhere. Frames are processed in memory and discarded.

Photos

If you take or choose a progress photo, it is stored on your device and shown back to you on the block screen. It is not uploaded.

Notifications

Byre schedules notifications locally on your device, for example to tell you a focus session has ended. We do not operate a push server and cannot send you messages.

Purchases

Subscriptions and Bailout packs are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

To know whether a purchase is active, Byre uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt from Apple along with a randomly generated identifier for your installation. This is used only to determine what you have bought and to restore purchases on a new device. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or usage-tracking SDKs are included in the app
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No sale or sharing of personal information
  • No tracking across other apps or websites

Children

Byre is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because your information stays on your device, you control it directly: Delete Account inside the app erases everything, as does deleting the app. If you have questions about data held by Apple or RevenueCat in connection with a purchase, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
