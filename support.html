Common questions

Blocking isn't working

Byre needs Screen Time permission to block apps. Open iOS Settings → Screen Time and confirm Byre is allowed, then reopen the app and choose your apps again under My apps.

I'm locked out and I need my phone

Every block screen has an Emergency button in the top corner. It is free, unlimited, and needs no challenge. If you want blocking off entirely, turn it off in Settings inside the app, or delete the app.

The camera challenge isn't counting my reps

The camera needs to see the joints involved — for squats that means your knees and hips in frame. Prop the phone so your whole body is visible, and stand back. If it still can't see you, use Tap them in yourself on the same screen; it is always available.

Restoring a purchase

Open the plan screen and tap Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so use the same one you bought with.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Deleting your data

Everything Byre stores is on your device. Settings → Delete Account erases it, and so does deleting the app.
